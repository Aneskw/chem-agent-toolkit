interpretation. Select deeper checks from its material risks:

   sourced instructions. Do not install, compile or execute tools.

   state. Verify quantitative interpretation conditions such as normalization, calibration,
   statistical degrees of freedom or candidate coverage. A returned statistic alone does not
   establish meaningful inference. Check glue as well as upstream calls.

   accidental dependence on example values, including materially different promised modes. Check

   avoidable burden. Do not demand scripts, file counts or expansion beyond sound declared scope.
